Collections agent

An agent with one job: getting you paid.

Most late invoices aren't disputes. Nobody followed up. The agent follows up on every one, on time, every time, and tells you the moment a customer needs a call from you.

  1. 1
    Spots it. An invoice passes its due date. The agent reads the customer's history: always late, or out of character?
  2. 2
    Drafts it. A friendly reminder in your voice, with the invoice attached and a way to pay.
  3. 3
    You approve. One tap to send from your own email. Or set rules, like "send small reminders without asking".
  4. 4
    It keeps going. Follow-ups on your schedule. Promises to pay go in the cash forecast. Anything tricky comes back to you.

Questions

Will the collections agent email my customers without asking?

Not unless you tell it to. Every reminder waits for your OK by default. You can set rules later, like sending first reminders under a set amount on their own.

How do you set the price for Scale?

After a short call. It depends on your sales, how many transactions and locations you have, and how often you want to meet.

Do you do taxes?

No. We keep the books ready for your tax preparer and work alongside them. Tilo gives analysis, not tax, legal or investment advice.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Start with the app or bookkeeping and add collections or Scale when you need them.
