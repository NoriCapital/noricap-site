Nori is the better fit if

  • You own a running business with a year or more of books in QuickBooks or Xero
  • You want problems flagged and questions answered without building or maintaining anything
  • You're planning to borrow and want to know what a lender will see, with the package ready
  • You need a CFO's analysis, but not $3,000 or more a month of one

When something else fits better

Clockwork is the better fit if

  • You want a 5-year financial model and a 52-week cash forecast, and the budget fits $719 a month or more
  • You're an accounting firm building an advisory practice across many clients
  • You need headcount planning tied to payroll and HR systems, or SOC 2 certification today

Spreadsheets is the better fit if

  • You enjoy building models and have the time to keep them current
  • Your business is simple enough that a monthly look is enough
  • You need a one-off custom model

Fathom is the better fit if

  • You're an accounting firm producing branded reports for many clients at a low per-company price
  • You need consolidated reporting across entities or currencies
  • You use MYOB, Sage or QuickBooks Desktop

LivePlan is the better fit if

  • You're starting a business and need a written business plan
  • You want a guided plan writer with sample plans and a pitch
  • You need projections with little or no history

A fractional CFO is the better fit if

  • You're raising equity, preparing for a sale, or need someone to manage lender relationships
  • You need someone to run the finance function, not just analyze it
  • You need board reporting and negotiation

Using them together

Many businesses mix these. Advisors use Fathom or Clockwork for client reporting while the client uses Nori day to day. LivePlan writes the plan for a new venture; Nori shows how the existing business is actually doing. A fractional CFO's hours go further when the forecast, reports and lender package are already built, and you can invite your CFO or accountant to Nori free.
