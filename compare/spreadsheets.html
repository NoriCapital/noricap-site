The hidden cost of spreadsheets

Most spreadsheet problems aren't formula errors. They're timing: the forecast was right when it was built and wrong three weeks later, because a customer paid late or a supplier raised prices. A spreadsheet only knows what someone last typed into it.

Many owners keep both. Nori does the recurring work from the books, and the spreadsheet stays for one-off projects, like pricing a new product line. The 13-week cash forecast template is free either way.
