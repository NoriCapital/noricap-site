Nori vs Fathom.

Fathom is a well-regarded reporting, forecasting and consolidation app, popular with accounting firms that produce management reports for clients. Nori is built for the owner: it reads the books, says what needs a decision, and gets the business ready to borrow.

Nori is the better fit if

  • You own the business and want answers and warnings, not a report to interpret
  • You're planning to borrow and want coverage worked out and the package built
  • You want a weekly cash forecast without building one

Fathom is the better fit if

  • You're an accounting firm producing branded reports for many clients at a low per-company price
  • You need consolidated reporting across several entities or currencies
  • You use MYOB, Sage or QuickBooks Desktop

Can you use both?

Yes. Some advisors use Fathom for client reporting, and their clients use Nori day to day for cash, alerts and funding. Nori invites the client's accountant free, so the advisor sees the same numbers the owner does.
