How Tilo works

From your business name to your own numbers in a few minutes. This is what you'll see along the way.

Step 1 · about a minute

Tell us about your business

Your business name, your website and what you do. Then create your account. No card yet.

Step 2 · about two minutes

Add your numbers, your way

  • Connect QuickBooks or Xero. Tilo imports 24 months and refreshes daily, read-only.
  • Upload a spreadsheet. An Excel or CSV export from any accounting system.
  • Answer 10 quick questions. Rough figures are fine. No files needed.

Step 3 · right away

See your first results

Your profit, how long your cash lasts and the ratios worth watching, before you add a card. Like what you see? Start the 7-day free trial to open everything.

Step 4 · every day after

Open your dashboard

Sales, profit, cash and what you could borrow, with every ratio explained. Connected books add issues sized in dollars, a 13-week cash forecast, the Monday email and Ask Tilo.

Built for business owners. Not financial experts.

No jargon, no endless spreadsheets, no sugarcoating. Just a clearer picture and what to do next.

Who's behind it

Built by someone who has sat on the lender's side of the table.

Tilo was founded by a former Wall Street M&A banker and private equity investor who has spent years deciding what a business is worth and how much debt it can carry.

  • What lenders check, built in: coverage, adjusted EBITDA, working capital, a debt schedule.
  • Every figure traceable to the transactions it came from.

Questions go straight to the founder: hello@heytilo.ai

Your data

Private by default.

  • The app only reads. Nothing is posted, edited or deleted in your books, unless you hire our bookkeepers to keep them.
  • No money moves. Tilo has no access to bank accounts, payroll or tax filings.
  • Delete any time from Settings. We don't sell your data.

Works with QuickBooks Online, Xero, and exports from any accounting system.
