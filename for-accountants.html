How it works

1. Your client adds Nori

They connect QuickBooks Online or Xero with read-only access, or upload exports. Nori never changes the books.

2. They invite you, free

From Settings, the owner invites you by email. Advisors and team members cost nothing extra; the owner's subscription covers the company.

3. One login, every client

Switch between client companies from one account. Each company's data stays separate.

4. Start meetings with answers

The monthly report, budget vs actual and open issues are ready before you walk in.

What you get for each client

  • Issues sized in dollars: past-due customers, supplier prices rising faster than sales, possible duplicate payments, bills that look like they haven't been entered, and customer concentration.
  • A monthly CFO report with commentary, and budget vs actual with the drivers of each miss.
  • A 13-week cash forecast with a warning before cash drops below the client's minimum.
  • Customer retention: customers kept and lost, and revenue retention year over year.
  • A lender package when a client needs funding: adjusted EBITDA with add-backs, debt schedule, coverage before and after the loan, the largest loan at 1.25x, a 12-month projection, PDF and Excel, and a private link for the lender.
  • Ask Nori: plain-English answers from the client's books, between your meetings.

What stays with you

Nori doesn't keep books, file taxes or give tax advice, and it never posts to the ledger. It reads the books you keep and turns them into questions worth asking. The judgment, and the client relationship, stay yours.
