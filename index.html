Getting started

What is Nori?

Nori is your AI-powered financial partner. It reads the books you export from QuickBooks or Xero, flags where money is leaking with a dollar figure on each issue, writes a monthly CFO report, and answers questions about your numbers.

What do I upload?

A transaction-level profit and loss report, ideally covering the last 24 months, plus an optional receivables aging report. QuickBooks and Xero exports work as they are, and the app shows you exactly where to click. From any other system or a spreadsheet, fill in our simple template. Direct connections to your accounting system are on the way.

How long does it take?

Minutes. As soon as you upload, Nori works out your margins, spending, customers and the issues worth your attention. Upload again any time to refresh.

What if my books are messy?

Most are. Nori tells you how much spending it couldn't place, and uncategorized spending is often a finding in its own right.

Using Nori

Where do Nori's numbers come from?

Every figure is calculated directly from the transactions you uploaded, and each issue links to the transactions behind it. Nori's AI explains the numbers; it doesn't invent them.

Can my team or my accountant use it?

Yes. Invite people from Settings. Members can see everything and ask Nori questions; only the owner can upload or delete data.

What does it cost?

Nori is free during early access, with no credit card. Before paid plans start, you'll hear from us first, and you can delete your data at any time.

Is this tax or investment advice?

No. Nori finds and sizes operating improvements in your own numbers. For tax, legal and investment decisions, keep working with your CPA and advisors.

Trust and data

Will this change anything in my books?

No. Nori only reads the files you upload. It never posts, edits or deletes entries, moves money, or files anything.

Who can see my data?

Only you and the people you invite. Each company's data is kept separate, and we don't sell it or share it with other customers.
