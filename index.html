Getting started

What is Nori?

Nori is your AI-powered financial partner. It reads the books you export from QuickBooks or Xero, flags where money is leaking with a dollar figure on each issue, writes a monthly CFO report, and answers questions about your numbers.

What do I upload?

A transaction-level export from your accounting system, ideally covering the last 24 months: Profit and Loss Detail from QuickBooks, Account Transactions from Xero, or the general ledger from NetSuite, Sage, Wave or any other system. A spreadsheet with a date, an account and an amount works too. You can also add a receivables aging report and a balance sheet, which the lender package uses. Or connect QuickBooks Online or Xero directly, and Nori keeps your numbers, including the balance sheet, up to date every day.

How long does it take?

Minutes. As soon as you upload, Nori works out your margins, spending, customers and the issues worth your attention. Upload again any time to refresh.

What if my books are messy?

Most are. Nori tells you how much spending it couldn't place, and uncategorized spending is often a finding in its own right.

Am I too small for Nori?

If you keep your books in QuickBooks, Xero or another accounting system and you have customers and suppliers to keep track of, Nori has something to work with. It works best with at least a year of history. With only a few months of books, trends and the cash forecast will be rougher, and Nori says so.

How does the cash forecast work?

Nori starts from today's bank balance (read from QuickBooks, or entered by you), then works out week by week when customers are likely to pay what they owe, what your usual sales will bring in, and when your regular bills fall due. You can add what your profit and loss doesn't show, like loan payments, owner withdrawals, taxes and bills not yet entered. If cash looks set to fall below your minimum, Nori flags the week.

Using Nori

Do I need to set anything up?

No. Connect QuickBooks Online or Xero, or upload an export, and Nori builds the cash forecast, reports and lender package itself. There are no templates to fill in and no model to maintain. You can add what your books don't show, like loan payments or owner draws, whenever you like.

How is Nori different from Clockwork, Fathom or LivePlan?

Nori is built for the owner of a running business and costs $199 a month: it tells you what needs a decision, answers questions from your books, and builds the package a lender asks for. Clockwork focuses on 5-year models and advisory firms, from $719 a month; Fathom on reports accountants produce for clients; LivePlan on writing a business plan. The full side-by-side comparison is at noricap.com/compare.

Where do Nori's numbers come from?

Every figure is calculated directly from the transactions you uploaded, and each issue links to the transactions behind it. Nori's AI explains the numbers; it doesn't invent them.

Can my team or my accountant use it?

Yes, at no extra cost. Invite people from Settings. Members can see everything and ask Nori questions; only the owner can upload or delete data.

What does it cost?

$199 a month per company, after a 7-day free trial. You add a card to start, you won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime in Settings. Your team and your accountant can join at no extra cost. Accountants and advisors with 5 or more client companies can ask about partner pricing.

Is this tax or investment advice?

No. Nori finds and sizes operating improvements in your own numbers. For tax, legal and investment decisions, keep working with your CPA and advisors.

Does Nori replace my accountant or a CFO?

No. Your accountant keeps the books and handles tax. Nori reads those books and turns them into answers, warnings and a forecast. Many owners use Nori between meetings with their accountant or a part-time CFO, so those meetings start with the answers.

No. Nori only reads your books. It never posts, edits or deletes entries, moves money, or files anything.
