Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026 (billing added)

This policy explains what information Nori Cap ("we," "us") collects through noricap.com and the Nori app at app.noricap.com (together, "Nori"), how we use it, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. Questions go to pa@noricap.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your name, email address and password. We store passwords only as a one-way hash; we can't see them.
  • When you sign in, and the companies you belong to and your role in each.

Your company's financial data

  • Files you upload, such as a Profit and Loss Detail report or an A/R aging report. We read each file once and store the transactions and balances in it (dates, accounts, customer and vendor names, memos, reference numbers and amounts). We don't keep the original file.
  • Connected accounting systems. If you connect QuickBooks Online or Xero, Nori reads the same kinds of records directly, with your permission: your chart of accounts, transactions, invoices and bills, customer and vendor names, and receivables. Nori never changes anything in your accounting system. The access keys are stored encrypted, and disconnecting from the Upload page removes them and revokes Nori's access.
  • Company details you enter, such as the company name, industry and the minimum cash you want to keep.
  • What you create in Nori: questions you ask Nori and its answers, decisions you record, the status of issues Nori flags, and monthly report commentary.

Billing

If you subscribe, Stripe collects your card details and billing address on its own secure page. We never see or store your full card number. We keep a reference to your Stripe customer and subscription and its status, such as trial, active or cancelled.

Forms on noricap.com

When you send us a question, a partner request or a funding request, we collect what you enter (such as your email, name, firm, what you're planning and a rough amount) and how you found us (the referring site or campaign tags in the link you followed).

Usage and technical information

  • Our hosting providers record standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.
  • We count how many questions each person asks Nori each day, to apply fair-use limits.
  • The app uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in. The marketing site uses Google Analytics; see "Cookies and analytics" below.

How we use it

  • To run Nori: compute your figures, flag issues, write reports and answer your questions.
  • To let the people you invite see your company's data, as you choose.
  • To bill your subscription and send you trial, receipt and payment notices.
  • To reply to your questions, partner requests and funding requests.
  • To keep Nori secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and meet legal obligations.
  • To understand which channels bring visitors to the website and improve it.

We don't use your company's financial data to advertise to you, and we don't sell it.

How Nori's AI works with your data

Nori's answers and report commentary are written by Claude, an AI model provided by Anthropic. When you ask Nori a question, the relevant figures and transactions from your company's data are sent to Anthropic to produce the answer. Your figures themselves are calculated by our own software, not by the AI.

  • Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic may not train its models on the content we send.
  • Anthropic may keep that content for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in its policies, and then deletes it.
  • Only data from the company you're working in is sent, and only for your question.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers. They may use your information only to provide their service to us.

  • Render hosts the Nori app and its database in the United States.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model behind Nori's answers and commentary.
  • Netlify hosts noricap.com and receives the forms on it.
  • Google provides website analytics and our email.
  • Intuit (QuickBooks) and Xero, if you choose to connect them, provide your records to Nori under your authorization.
  • Stripe processes subscription payments and keeps billing records.
  • Resend delivers Nori's account emails, such as password resets and billing notices.

We don't share your information with lenders, investors or other third parties without your permission, including if you send us a funding request. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger, financing or sale of our business, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information or your financial data.
  • We don't share one company's data with another company or customer.
  • We don't connect to your bank, move money, post or change accounting entries, or file anything on your behalf. We connect to your accounting system only if you choose to, and only to read.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is open. You can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Settings → Your data deletes a company's uploads, transactions, reports, chats and decisions, or the whole company.
  • Settings → Delete your account deletes your sign-in, your chats, and every company you own.

Deletion removes the data from our live database right away. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are removed as those backups expire. Billing records, such as invoices and payments, are kept by us and Stripe for as long as tax and accounting laws require, even after you delete your account. Form submissions are kept until you ask us to delete them; email pa@noricap.com.

Security

All traffic uses encryption in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as hashes, each company's data is kept separate and checked on every request, and access to production systems is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we'll tell you without undue delay.

Cookies and analytics

The Nori app uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in; it is not used for tracking. The marketing site at noricap.com uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use it. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of certain uses. Most of this you can do yourself in Settings. For anything else, email us and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Nori is a business service and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle data you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
